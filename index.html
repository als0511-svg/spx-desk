Playbook — when to click buy

  1. 6:10 AM PDT — Manual Refresh. No order. Draw the two watch numbers.
  2. 6:30–7:00 — Let the 9:30–10:00 ET range print. Still WAIT.
  3. 7:00–8:30 AM PDT — Manual Refresh. OR high = call line. OR low = put line. Last look 8:30. After 8:30, no new trade.
  4. PLACE CALL only if price holds above OR high for 10 minutes before 8:30 AM PDT, no whipsaw, not an event day, VIX under 24.
  5. PLACE PUT only if price holds below OR low for 10 minutes before 8:30 AM PDT, same filters.
  6. If both sides of the range get tagged, or the hold fails — stay on WAIT. Flat is a position.
This does not raise the win rate of lottery tickets. Default strike is ~0.25–0.30 delta (near the money), not 0.05 delta. Skip is still the most common output.
